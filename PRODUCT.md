# ALOPAK — Product

## What it is
B2B website for ALOPAK Sp. z o.o., a Polish aluminum packaging producer (Ostrzeszów, since 1997). Primary goal: qualified quote requests.

## Audience
Procurement, production, packaging and wholesale buyers needing aluminum cans, food foil, or salon foil.

## Offer (confirmed)
1. Aluminum cans / food packaging cans
2. Food foil
3. Hairdressing foil

## Non-negotiables
- No invented specs, certificates, MOQ, prices, clients, or timelines
- No construction imagery (references are layout-only)
- No generic AI product mockups presented as ALOPAK photos
- Contact and legal data only from confirmed sources (alopak.com.pl)

## Success
Visitor understands the three categories in seconds and can send a quote with category, volume notes, and optional attachment.
